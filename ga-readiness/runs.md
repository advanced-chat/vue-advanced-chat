## Evidence rules

- Record exact commit, package version, environment, scenario, and result.
- Prefer CI URLs, package versions, provenance records, attached logs, and
  immutable artifacts.
- Candidate artifact evidence also records workflow run/artifact ID, filename,
  SHA-256, and npm integrity from the identity manifest.
- `Tested locally` without environment and output details is not release
  evidence.
- Verification evidence must postdate the fix commit and target candidate
  bytes.
- `Inconclusive` is valid evidence but cannot advance a lifecycle stage.
- Any candidate-affecting change marks prior candidate evidence stale rather
  than erasing it.
- Blocker and High closure requires evidence recorded by an independent
  verifier.
